Every post here falls into one of four types:

| Type | What you get |
|------|--------------|
| **Issue** | A real problem, its root cause, and the fix |
| **New Feature** | A platform feature explained with a minimal working example |
| **Code Bit** | A short snippet worth keeping |
| **Full Solution** | A complete, deployable piece of work |

## How I post daily

```bash
node tools/posts.js new "My post title" --type issue --tags "AL, Reports"
```

That creates today's file from a template and refreshes the post list. Fill it in, commit, and push.
